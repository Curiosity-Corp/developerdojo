## The five checks, for mentoring
- **Provenance and rights:** the learner owns their work; nothing from it is copied here.
- **Security:** no credentials or access tokens were shared to give feedback; access granted for the review is removed afterwards.
- **Reproduction:** each observation points to a file, a check result or a moment in the session the learner can find.
- **Independent review:** the reviewer is not the learner's own coach on this work and has no conflict (`docs/certificates.md` section 3). Feedback is formative: it is not a certificate, belt or rank decision.
- **History:** the learner's chosen next step and follow-up date; what was kept and what was deleted when the record closes.
