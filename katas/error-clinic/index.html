Katas / First steps · About 10 minutes

Spot the AI mistakes.

Catch three errors in a report and turn it into something trustworthy.

Start this kata See the result

01 / See it first

Here’s what you can make

The evidence · fictional practice data

Week 1
120 visits · 12 sign-ups

Week 2
100 visits · 15 sign-ups

The draft · find three problems

“Visits grew by 20%. The sign-up rate dropped from 10% to 5%. Customers love the new checkout because it is faster.”
Reveal the checks
  1. Visits fell by 20 out of 120, about 16.7%; they did not grow.
  2. Sign-up rate rose from 12 ÷ 120 = 10% to 15 ÷ 100 = 15%.
  3. The dataset contains no customer feedback or timing evidence, so the claim about customer opinions and speed is unsupported.

A corrected report: Visits fell from 120 to 100, while the sign-up rate rose from 10% to 15%. The data does not explain why, or show what customers think.

Give yourself one check for each of the three problems you found. Use the explanation to revisit anything you missed.

This is a fictional teaching example created for the kata. Neither the numbers nor the draft describe actual customers or results.

04 / Pick your setup

Use the device you have

Choose one path. You can do the chat exercise without installing a command line tool.

Phone · ChatGPT app

Open the ChatGPT app, start a new chat, and paste the prompt. Ask the assistant for the requested result. Some account-available plugins can be used on mobile; desktop-only plugins cannot. Plugins are optional here. ChatGPT plugin guide.

Phone · Claude app

Open the Claude app, start a new chat, and paste the prompt. Ask for the requested result. Remote connectors are optional; local desktop extensions do not run on the phone. Claude connectors guide.

Open ChatGPT or Claude in a browser and paste the prompt. Alternatively, install one CLI in PowerShell using an official command below. 05 / Check and remix

Make sure it works for you

  1. Check the arithmetic yourself: 20 ÷ 120 is about 16.7%, and 15 ÷ 100 is 15%.
  2. Look for claims about why the numbers changed. The practice data does not establish a cause.
  3. Ask the assistant to correct anything it cannot support and to separate facts from assumptions.
  4. Remix with a small dataset you are allowed to share; keep private information out of the prompt.
