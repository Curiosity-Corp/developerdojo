Kata recipes

Each recipe starts with the outcome, then gives the stack, prompts, checks, failures and ways to adapt it. Anything that has not been checked says so.

Recipes

  • Inspect a daily weather record: dates, units, gaps and flagsAGR-001 · Draft

    Open a year of real daily weather from NOAA and write down what it holds before you use it: station, dates, units, missing values, and every measurement, quality and source flag.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the agriculture pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Estimate reference evapotranspiration with FAO-56 from temperature dataAGR-002 · Draft

    Reproduce FAO's worked examples for extraterrestrial radiation and Penman-Monteith reference evapotranspiration, then compute daily ET0 for a real year from temperature alone and compare it with the Hargreaves equation.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the agriculture pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Estimate a crop's net irrigation need from crop coefficients and effective rainAGR-003 · Draft

    Turn reference evapotranspiration into a maize crop's water need with FAO-56 crop coefficients by growth stage, subtract effective rain with FAO's simple monthly formula, and report the net irrigation need by month.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the agriculture pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Agriculture Randori: an unseen change of assumptionAGR-R01 · Draft

    Start from the maize estimate; a partner reveals one changed assumption after you begin. Predict the direction and rough size of the change, then recompute and explain the difference.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the agriculture pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Inspect an IFC model: schema, units, spatial tree and relationshipsBIM-001 · Draft

    Open a real IFC file with IfcOpenShell and write down what it declares: schema, units, the spatial tree, each element with its GlobalId, and how the opening and window are related to the wall.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the bim pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Read the property sets and find what is missingBIM-002 · Draft

    List every property set on the wall and window with its values and types, and mark the properties that exist but hold no value.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the bim pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Check a model against IDS requirementsBIM-003 · Draft

    Read a practice IDS file, run it against the sample with IfcTester, and explain the one requirement that fails.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the bim pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Change the model and check it againBIM-004 · Draft

    Fix the failed requirement with an IfcOpenShell edit, keep every GlobalId, then rerun the IDS, schema and geometry checks on the edited copy.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the bim pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Build a 3D mesh and check its shapeBIM-005 · Draft

    Turn the IFC elements into a triangle mesh with IfcOpenShell, check the mesh from its triangles alone, and name what the mesh no longer knows.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the bim pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Trace quantities to the model objectsBIM-006 · Draft

    Compute wall, opening and window quantities from the IFC model, trace each one to its source entities, unit and assumption, and cross-check it against the built geometry.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the bim pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Find a clash and raise it as a BCF issueBIM-007 · Draft

    Add a practice duct to the model, find where it clashes, check the clash with a second route, and raise it as an issue in a BCF file that validates and reads back.

    Verified.Last verified 22 November 2026 by the bim pathway maintainer. Next check by 20 February 2027.

  • Map the model to a sequence and an unpriced cost planBIM-008 · Draft

    Map the model's elements to construction tasks, derive each dependency from a relationship in the model or an open issue, and build an unpriced cost plan whose lines cite traced quantities.

    Verified.Last verified 22 November 2026 by the bim pathway maintainer. Next check by 20 February 2027.

  • BIM Randori: an unseen requirement changeBIM-R01 · Draft

    Start from the edited model; a partner reveals one new requirement card after you begin. Check, fix and recheck without breaking what already passed.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the bim pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Specialist BIM Randori: a changed requirement, format or toolBIM-R02 · Draft

    A specialist partner changes one thing after you start, a requirement, an exchange format or a tool, and you show what changes in the coordination evidence and the review package.

    Verified.Last verified 22 November 2026 by the bim pathway maintainer. Next check by 20 February 2027.

  • Build a working prototype, then change one stack variableFND-001 · Draft

    Start from a working prototype. Have an AI assistant rebuild it from a fixed brief, score the result with an independent rubric, then change exactly one part of the stack and compare.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the foundation kata maintainer (not yet assigned). Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Inspect a published research record: columns, units, gaps and internal consistencySCI-001 · Draft

    Open NASA’s GISTEMP global temperature table and write down what it holds before you use it: years, columns, the unit and base period, every unavailable cell, and whether the annual means agree with their months.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the science pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Evaluate measurement uncertainty: Type A evaluation, correlated inputs and an uncertainty budgetSCI-002 · Draft

    Reproduce the GUM’s worked example of correlated measurements: evaluate Type A standard uncertainties from five sets of observations, propagate them with their correlations, and write an uncertainty budget a reviewer can check line by line.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the science pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Fit a line by least squares and report its parameters with standard uncertaintiesSCI-003 · Draft

    Reproduce the GUM’s thermometer calibration line, then fit a straight line through 30 years of NASA’s global temperature record and report its slope with a standard uncertainty, saying plainly what that uncertainty assumes.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the science pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Science Randori: an unseen change of assumptionSCI-R01 · Draft

    Start from the 1991-2020 fit; a partner reveals one changed assumption after you begin. Predict how the slope and its standard uncertainty move, then recompute and explain the difference.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the science pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Map a sitemap with branches and error statesUX-001 · Draft

    Map every screen and state of a small SaaS flow, including errors, empty states, a dialog and the way back, before you draw a single screen.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the ux pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Draw low-fidelity screens and wire the flowUX-002 · Draft

    Turn the sitemap into greyscale screens at phone size, wired into a clickable prototype that reaches every state, errors included.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the ux pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Define design tokens for colour, type and spacingUX-003 · Draft

    Name the colours, type sizes and spacing of the design once, as design tokens in a JSON file, and check every text and control colour pair for contrast.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the ux pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Build components from tokensUX-004 · Draft

    Turn the repeated parts of your screens into components (buttons, fields, the error summary, the dialog) built from your tokens, so one change updates every screen.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the ux pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.

  • Implement the screens and check accessibilityUX-005 · Draft

    Build the screens as a working web page from the same flow and tokens, then prove keyboard use, screen reader structure, contrast and reflow with automated checks.

    Verified.Last verified 21 November 2026 by the ux pathway maintainer. Next check by 19 February 2027.
