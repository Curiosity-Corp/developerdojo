Katas / First steps · About 10 minutes

Turn an idea into a plan.

Make a clear one-page brief for something you want to bring into the world.

Start this kata See the result

01 / See it first

Here’s what you can make

Example brief

Neighborhood seed swap

Goal: Help neighbors share spare seeds and try growing something new.

For: People nearby who want to grow plants but may not need a whole packet of seeds.

First steps: Ask a few neighbors if the idea is useful. Find out what seeds people could share. Explore a suitable way to exchange them.

Small test: Describe the idea to a few people and note whether they would offer or take seeds.

Check: We do not yet know if people have spare seeds, or where an exchange could happen.

Question: Where would neighbors most easily hear about the idea?

This fictional example brief was created for the kata with AI assistance. It illustrates the format; the seed swap and its results are not presented as real.

03 / Your ingredients

A simple stack

Harness
ChatGPT or Claude app, or either service in a browser.
Model
A model available in your chosen chat. Access and limits depend on your account.
Instructions
The complete prompt above. Change the topic and constraints to fit your goal.
Context
Your idea and any non-sensitive details you choose to share.
Skills, CLI, MCP, plugins or connectors
None needed for the basic chat version. Optional ways to equip your setup are below.
Agent and verification
One chat assistant drafts; you inspect the result against the checks below and ask it to fix problems.

04 / Pick your setup

Use the device you have

Choose one path. You can do the chat exercise without installing a command line tool.

Phone · ChatGPT app

Open the ChatGPT app, start a new chat, and paste the prompt. Ask the assistant for the requested result. Some account-available plugins can be used on mobile; desktop-only plugins cannot. Plugins are optional here. ChatGPT plugin guide.

Phone · Claude app

Open the Claude app, start a new chat, and paste the prompt. Ask for the requested result. Remote connectors are optional; local desktop extensions do not run on the phone. Claude connectors guide.

Windows

Open ChatGPT or Claude in a browser and paste the prompt. Alternatively, install one CLI in PowerShell using an official command below. These commands download and execute installers; check the linked vendor instructions first. Sign in when prompted; eligibility and plans vary. Use a folder you are comfortable granting the tool access to.

powershell -ExecutionPolicy ByPass -c "irm https://chatgpt.com/codex/install.ps1 | iex"
codex

05 / Check and remix

Make sure it works for you

  1. Check that the goal, audience, three steps, one test and two assumptions are all present.
  2. Look for invented facts or promises. Ask the assistant to label or remove anything it cannot support.
  3. Replace the example idea with your own. Keep the same structure and add your real constraints.
  4. Ask: “What is the smallest safe test I could try first, and what would I learn from it?”

Remix: try the same prompt for a school club, an app idea or a creative project.
