# UX Randori cards (for the partner)

Pick one brief and one constraint. Read the brief aloud. Keep the constraint until the learner has drawn a first sitemap.

## Briefs
1. A library app: a member reserves a book, is told when it is ready, and can cancel.
2. A tool-lending shelf for neighbours: borrow a drill, return it, report it broken.
3. A class trip form: a parent gives permission, pays, and can change their answer.

Every brief below now has a worked answer on the pathway page. For a round nobody has answered, use the
unseen round there (round U): its brief, its sealed card and its review rubric.

## Hidden constraints
- Every screen must work with a keyboard only, and the order of focus must match the order of reading.
- The user may lose their connection at any step; nothing they typed may be lost.
- Half the users read English as a second language; no screen may have more than one action.
- The user must be able to undo the last action for 30 seconds.
- A second person (a parent, a manager) must approve before the action takes effect.
