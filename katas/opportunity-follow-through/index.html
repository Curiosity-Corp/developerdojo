Start free

Business kata · about 10 minutes

Turn an inquiry into a next step

Map the people, make the next action clear, and check the evidence before anyone reaches out.

Start with the example

The finished outcome

A lead you can actually follow

1 · Source
A fictional inquiry asks for demo information.

2 · People
The sender is known by role; the buyer and approver are unknown.

3 · Next step
Mira drafts an internal response by 12 May for review.

4 · Verify
No message, sale or revenue is documented.

This is a worked practice outcome made from invented records, not a real customer or an AI run. An inquiry is a lead to investigate; it is not a signed contract or earned revenue.

Ingredients: the three short records and prompt below, a place to save a draft, and your own judgment. Use any available assistant or trusted CLI harness; no skill, MCP server, plugin, CRM connection, paid tool or account is required for the paper exercise.

On a phone, use an assistant app or write it yourself. On Windows, macOS or Linux, use a browser or a CLI harness you already trust. Provider availability, account requirements and charges vary. This exercise does not install or connect anything.

Start here

Read the evidence

These records were invented for this practice and may be copied into your assistant. Each ID identifies a source; there are no real email addresses. For real inquiries, get permission before sharing information with an outside service.

  1. S1 · Inquiry. A fictional garden cooperative asks for information about a volunteer-scheduling demo. It has not stated a budget or purchase date. The sender agreed to a reply about this inquiry only.
  2. S2 · Internal planning note. Mira will draft a response for internal review by 12 May; the note does not state a year. The approving manager has not been named. No message has been sent.
  3. S3 · Privacy note. Do not share the inquiry with an outside AI service without permission. Further marketing and use of the cooperative's name in public material are not approved. The buyer or decision-maker is unknown.

Build the next-step record

  1. Map roles: the inquiry sender, the unknown buyer, the internal owner and the unknown approver. Mark missing names as unknown.
  2. Write the opportunity stage, one next action, its owner and date. Cite the source IDs and list what needs permission or clarification.
  3. Use the prompt below if you want an assistant. Compare every line with S1–S3 before treating it as a plan.
Or select and copy the text.
Reveal the independent check
  • Stage: an inquiry to investigate (S1); it is not a qualified deal or a sale.
  • Relationship map: inquiry sender—name and role beyond sender unknown (S1); buyer unknown (S3); internal owner—Mira (S2); approving manager unknown (S2).
  • Next action: Mira drafts a response for internal review by 12 May (S2); the year is unknown. A reply may be relevant to the inquiry, but the records do not say that a response was approved or sent.
  • Evidence and limits: the budget, purchase date and revenue are unknown; no deal or meeting is documented (S1–S3). The sender agreed to an inquiry reply only; further marketing and public use of the name are not approved (S1, S3). Do not put the inquiry into an external AI service without permission (S3).

Adapt

Make it useful in your field

Try the same source → people → next step → verify pattern for a partnership, procurement notice or client request. Record the public source or authorized record, who can make a decision, the relationship you need to develop, and the next follow-up. Do not guess contact details, claim a response or count potential value as revenue. Check communication permissions, client approval and applicable rules before outreach.

Practice with HubSpot Academy’s Inbound Sales course for more on finding and engaging buyers. HubSpot describes it as free; enrollment requires sign-up and availability can change. This kata does not grant its certification or make a CRM connection.
