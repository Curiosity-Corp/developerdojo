Evidence kata · about 10 minutes

Check an activity report fairly

Turn an overconfident report into an evidence record a person can question and correct.

Inspect the evidence

The outcome · fictional worked example

A report with room for the missing story

A workspace export records two messages and one document revision. An organizer’s note records a stand-up. The teammate says some work happened outside the workspace; that account has not yet been checked. The sources are fictional practice material, not a real monitoring report or an AI run.

What the records say

The export records two messages and one revision (A1). The organizer’s note records the teammate joining a stand-up (B1). A separate account describes phone and offline work (C1), which is not yet verified.

What remains unknown

The export omits phone, external email and offline work and does not measure attendance or hours (A2). The meeting note gives no duration (B1). Neither record establishes overall contribution or performance.

What to do next

Show the person the draft, invite their account and corrections, request only permitted supporting evidence, and reconcile the sources. A responsible human reviews the result; do not automate personnel decisions.

Correction: “did not work,” “missed the stand-up” and “made no contribution” are not supported. The meeting note conflicts with the missed-meeting claim. A record’s absence from one system is not proof that an activity did not occur.

Verify and adapt

Ask before you conclude

  1. Check scope: write down the period and which systems and activities the export covers. The sample does not measure hours, location or meeting attendance.
  2. Reconcile: the organizer’s note contradicts “missed the stand-up”; check the underlying note and resolve any disagreement rather than choosing a convenient record.
  3. Listen and correct: ask the person for their account and any evidence they can share appropriately. Label unverified statements and give them a chance to explain or challenge errors.
  4. Protect and review: restrict access to sensitive records, avoid copying personal or client information into unapproved tools, and follow the relevant workplace policies and applicable rules. A person, not an automated report, must handle consequential decisions.
Quick check: does the export prove a teammate worked zero hours?

No. The export does not measure hours and excludes some work. Ask for context and check appropriate records.
