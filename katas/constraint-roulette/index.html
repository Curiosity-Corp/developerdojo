Change the rules

Choose your own tools and approach. Your challenge is to deliver and verify a useful result under a new constraint.

Your mission

Develop a short, usable plan for a neighborhood event. Explain the audience, the first three actions, one uncertainty and how you would check whether the plan works. Do not invent permission, attendance or results.

Add a constraint

Choose one below, or let the button pick. You decide the stack and the method.

  • Only a phone is available.
  • The audience needs a plain-language version.
  • The venue is unavailable and has not been replaced.
  • The tool you planned to use is unavailable.

How to check yourself

  1. Does the result satisfy the chosen constraint?
  2. Are assumptions clearly separated from facts?
  3. Can another person follow the next steps and check the result?
  4. What would you change if your first tool or approach failed?

This is practice, not an automatically graded belt. Try Decision Replay or explore more katas.
