Finance · Pathway

From a loan’s terms to a checked annual percentage rate.

Four katas on the method US law prescribes for the cost of a loan, and the regulation’s own worked examples. Each step has a file you can open and a result that CI computes again on every pipeline.

Start with FIN-001Download the schedule

01 / The files

One regulation, its own examples, every number rerun

The method is Regulation Z, the US Truth in Lending rule (12 CFR part 1026). Appendix J sets out the actuarial method for the annual percentage rate of a loan, with worked examples and the rates they give; 1026.22 says how close a disclosed rate must be. The script reproduces the appendix’s examples before it computes anything else.

  • 12 CFR 1026.22 (XML, as the eCFR served it), unchanged. The script reads the tolerances from it.
  • The payment schedule (CSV) and its disclosure figures (JSON).
  • The annual percentage rates (JSON) and the tolerance check (JSON).
  • The result summary (Markdown) and every result under the manifest, with the method and the input hash.
  • The pathway script (Python 3.12, standard library only, MIT), so you can rerun every number yourself.
  • README: sources, terms, how to rerun the checks, and what they do not cover.

02 / The steps

Four katas, in order

Each kata has a brief, prompts, checks and the evidence for the checks the Dojo has run. They are drafts: the Dojo built the example and CI checks it, and no learner has run them yet. Do the foundations first: Compare AI instructions and Spot a context trap.

  1. FIN-001 Amortize a loan to the cent

    A month-by-month schedule for one of the regulation’s examples, and the four figures a disclosure shows.

  2. FIN-002 Compute the annual percentage rate

    Count the unit-periods from the dates, then solve the regulation’s equation by its own iteration and reproduce every worked example.

  3. FIN-003 Check the rate against its tolerance

    Read the tolerance from the regulation’s text, work out which disclosed rates count as accurate, and test a common shortcut.

  4. FIN-R01 Finance Randori: an unseen change

    A partner reveals one changed term after you start; predict whether the rate moves beyond the tolerance, then recompute.

The tolerance (FIN-003)

12 CFR 1026.22(a)(2), as served: “(2) As a general rule, the annual percentage rate shall be considered accurate if it is not more than 1/8 of 1 percentage point above or below the annual percentage rate determined in accordance with paragraph (a)(1) of this section.” By (a)(3), an odd first period and an irregular first or final payment do not make a transaction irregular, so every example uses 0.125 percentage point. The last two columns show what happens when the odd days are ignored and the first payment is treated as exactly one unit-period after the advance.

04 / The tools and sources

No packages, public sources

The script uses only the Python 3.12 standard library, so it runs offline with nothing to install. A spreadsheet works for checking one row of the schedule by hand. The Armory lists the stack recipe Python standard library.

  • Regulation Z, Appendix J: the general equation (b)(8), counting unit-periods between dates (b)(5), the iteration (b)(9) and the worked examples in (c)(1) and (c)(3).
  • 12 CFR 1026.22: what the annual percentage rate is and the tolerances in (a)(2) and (a)(3).
  • 12 CFR 1026.18: the disclosure figures, and 12 CFR 1026.4: what a real finance charge includes.
  • The eCFR, where the served copy of 1026.22 comes from. It is an editorial compilation, not the official legal edition.

05 / What is checked

Verified, and not verified

Verified

  • The regulation text is the file the eCFR served for 2026-11-23, unchanged (SHA-256 ed61b93aa82d…, checked by the site’s tests), and the script reads the tolerances from it.
  • The script reproduces 20 published values from Appendix J’s worked examples (the iteration of paragraph (b)(9), and the unit-periods and annual percentage rates of the examples in (c)(1) and (c)(3)) and refuses to write any result if one differs. (evidence)
  • Ignoring the odd days before the first payment moves the annual percentage rate beyond the 1/8-point tolerance in 5 of 7 examples. (evidence)
  • On every pipeline, CI reruns the script with Python 3.12 and fails if any output differs from the files on this page. (evidence)

Not verified

  • Any real loan, fee or disclosure. The examples are the regulation’s own, with dates in 1978; nothing here says that a real disclosure or calculation tool meets Regulation Z.
  • What belongs in a real finance charge (12 CFR 1026.4). The worked example has no fees, so its amount financed is the amount advanced.
  • Multiple advances, irregular payment series, the United States Rule method, open-end credit and the extra tolerances for mortgage loans (1026.22(a)(4) and (a)(5)).
  • State law, and any rule outside Regulation Z that a real loan is also subject to.
  • Use by real learners: no learner has run these katas yet.

Professional review: None. No compliance specialist, lawyer or accountant has reviewed this pathway; a real loan disclosure needs the review its lender’s compliance function and counsel require.

What this field adds to the shared foundations

Terms of the field

Amount financed
The credit provided to the borrower: the loan amount, plus other amounts financed that are not finance charges, minus any prepaid finance charge (12 CFR 1026.18(b)).
Finance charge
The cost of the credit as a dollar amount (12 CFR 1026.18(d)); in the worked example, the total of payments minus the amount financed.
Total of payments
What the borrower will have paid after making every scheduled payment (12 CFR 1026.18(h)).
Annual percentage rate
The cost of credit as a yearly rate, relating the amount and timing of what the borrower receives to the amount and timing of the payments (12 CFR 1026.22(a)(1)).
Unit-period
The period between payments that occurs most often in a transaction, such as a month or a week; the annual percentage rate is the rate per unit-period times the unit-periods per year (Appendix J).
Odd first period
A first period that is longer or shorter than a unit-period, counted as whole unit-periods t plus a fraction f (Appendix J).
Tolerance
How far a disclosed annual percentage rate may differ from the computed one and still count as accurate: 1/8 of 1 percentage point in a regular transaction (12 CFR 1026.22(a)(2)).

Procedures practised

  1. Amortize a closed-end loan to the cent and state its disclosure figures (FIN-001)
  2. Count unit-periods from the dates and compute the annual percentage rate by the actuarial method of Appendix J (FIN-002)
  3. Check a disclosed annual percentage rate against the Regulation Z tolerance (FIN-003)

Standards cited, by version

  • Regulation Z (Truth in Lending), 12 CFR part 1026, Appendix J and 1026.22, eCFR for 2026-11-23; Appendix J and 1026.22 unchanged there since 2017-01-01 (read against in FIN-001, FIN-002, FIN-003, FIN-R01)
