One foundation, many fields

Development crosses industries.

Your foundation in harnesses, models, instructions, tools and verification can travel with you. Each field adds its own skills, standards and forms of review.

  • Digital products & UXJourneys, prototypes, software and accessible experiences.
  • 3D, BIM & constructionEditable models, coordination and the built environment.
  • Robotics & manufacturingPhysical systems, simulation, design and production.
  • Science & healthResearch, models and evidence with qualified review.
  • Energy & agricultureDevelop work for the natural and built world.
  • Media & businessCreative work, communication and useful workflows.

These are examples of where the Dojo can grow; a full set of katas is not yet available for every field. Explore the fields

Training path

Grow as a developer.

Practice, adapt and teach. Our existing product training remains available as the Dojo grows into more fields.

  1. DeveloperBuild real products, learn product basics, roadmapping, agile, stakeholder meetings, and delivery.
  3. Master DeveloperOwn one product end to end, steer AI agents, run platform and launch work.
  5. SenseiCoach other developers, host live builds, review work, and help juniors grow.

The academy

What you'll learn.

Build shared development skills, then apply them with the expertise and review your chosen field requires.

  • Outcomes and evidence

    Define the goal, constraints and proof of success.

  • Field expertise

    Learn the tools, standards and reviews your field needs.

  • Instructions and context

    Give agents clear direction and the information they need.

  • Agent stacks

    Choose harnesses, models, skills and integrations for the work.

  • Verify and manage risk

    Check results, permissions, limitations and costs.

  • Practice and adapt

    Repeat a technique, learn from the evidence and apply it to a new goal.
