Developer path

  • Developer path: six weeks

    The six-week plan to own one product from first customer talk to a final Product Package.

  • Week 1: See the whole product

    Draw your product on one page, list every part, and start talking to customers.

  • Week 2: Know the people

    Interview users, write personas, map touch points and draw user and customer journeys.

  • Week 3: From problems to epics

    Turn the gaps in your journeys into a roadmap of 4 to 8 epics that you write yourself.

  • Week 4: Defend the roadmap

    Check dependencies, map stakeholders, defend your choices, and learn to steer AI agents with agent-ready epics.

  • Week 5: Brand, website and mockups

    Choose a brand name, publish a simple product website, and draw mockups of the next version.

  • Week 6: Catalogue, demo and final package

    Write your Product Catalogue, record a demo, and present your Product Package.

  • Product Package standard

    What the final Developer assessment contains and how to prepare it.

  • Product Package rubric

    The six skills and the scoring levels used to assess your Product Package.

  • Weekly cadence and common mistakes

    A simple weekly rhythm for an epic manager, and the mistakes to avoid.

Templates

  • Templates: how to use them

    Copy-ready templates for the Developer path and the Master Developer rank.

  • Template: Product brief

    A one-page brief that says why a product exists, who it is for and what success looks like.

  • Template: Personas and demographics

    A demographics sheet and a one-page persona, with known and guess marks.

  • Template: Touch points and journeys

    Three tables: touch-point inventory, user journey and customer journey, with a bridge to your repositories.

  • Template: Repository map

    One table that lists every repository your product needs, with owner, kind and status.

  • Template: Integrations register

    A table of every outside tool your product uses, who it serves and what data moves.

  • Template: Epic and roadmap

    Write one epic by hand and lay your epics out as a roadmap.

  • Template: Decision record

    A short record of a tool or design decision: what, why, alternatives, licence, exit plan.

  • Template: Catalogue entry

    A small file that describes one product or service: owner, tier, repos, dependencies and service levels.

  • Template: Change record

    A merge request template that records the reason, risk, recovery plan and checks for a change.

  • Template: Weekly note

    A short note that tells your sponsor or coach the truth about the week.
