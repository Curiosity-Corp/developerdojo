Guides

Decision-making

  • Frame the problem

    Turn a vague worry into one decision with an owner and a deadline, and write a problem statement that says who has the problem, what it is, why it matters and how you will know it is solved.

  • Ask better questions

    Map the people behind a decision, sort what you know from what you only believe, and turn the gaps into questions with an owner and a date, so you learn enough to decide and then stop.

  • Compare options and write the trade-off down

    Write your criteria before you look at the options, compare at least three choices including doing nothing, name the trade-off you accept and keep it all in a short decision record in git.

  • Decide with agents

    Brief an agent to prepare options and a comparison from evidence you supply, test what it returns with a written review, and make the decision yourself. The agent never decides.
