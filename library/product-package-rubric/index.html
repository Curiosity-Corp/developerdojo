Product Package rubric

The six skills and the scoring levels used to assess your Product Package.

Score each skill from 1 to 4. Write one example from your own work as evidence. Your coach scores the same skills. The gap between the two scores is where you learn most.

Target: level 3 on all six skills. No skill below level 2.

The six skills

Skill 1 Beginning 2 Developing 3 Competent 4 Strong
A. Whole-product view I know my own part only. I know most parts but miss links. My map shows every repo, service and integration and how they connect. I can predict how a change in one part hits the others.
B. Customers and personas I describe users in general words. Personas come from guesses. Personas use real evidence and separate user from customer. Personas change my priorities, and I can show the evidence.
C. Journeys and touch points I list screens. I map one journey with some touch points. I map user and customer journeys with pain points and owners. I find gaps nobody saw. Each gap links to a candidate epic.
D. Writing epics My epics are big tasks. Epics have a goal but no measure or scope limit. Each epic has problem, outcome, measure, scope in and out, dependencies and risks, in my words. My epics make others say “now I understand what we are doing and why”.
E. Roadmap and priorities A list with no order. An order with no reasons. Ordered by value, risk and dependency, with a reason for each place. I defend the order, say what I chose not to do, and adjust when facts change.
F. Hand-off to agents Agents often ask what I mean. Milestones are clear but epics are incomplete. Epics, dates and labels are correct. Milestones are clear enough for agents. Agents produce the right issues first time and I review them fast and well.

Honesty check (every week)

  • Did I write every epic and roadmap sentence myself?
  • Did I use AI only for allowed tasks and say so?
  • Can I explain each epic out loud without notes?

Package completeness (tick each)

  • Current state
  • Product website
  • Brand name
  • Product and service catalogue
  • Roadmap
  • Demo
  • Next-version mockups
  • Evidence of complete epic management

A missing part is a gap to fix. It is not a score.

Reading the result

  • Level 3 or above on all skills: pass.
  • Any skill at 2: write a plan to lift it, with your coach.
  • Any skill at 1: stop adding work and repair that skill first.

Self-review for the epic manager (ten questions)

Score 0 (no), 1 (partly), 2 (yes). Maximum 20.

  1. The top epic names a real user and a real number.
  2. I wrote the five core lines myself.
  3. The repo map was built by walking journeys.
  4. Every repo has an owner and an epic link.
  5. Each epic has acceptance lines I can check.
  6. Order and blocking links are set.
  7. Roadmap dates are true today.
  8. Out-of-scope items are written down.
  9. The weekly note went out every week.
  10. I talked to a user in the last two weeks.

17 to 20: strong. 12 to 16: fix the lowest three. Below 12: stop adding work and repair the plan.
