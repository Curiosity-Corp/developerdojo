The path from builder to Sensei

A clear step-by-step path for members who build, ship, and then give back to the community through coaching, live builds and reviews.

  1. DeveloperBuild real products, learn product basics, roadmapping, agile, stakeholder meetings, and delivery.
  3. Master DeveloperOwn one product end to end, steer AI agents, run platform and launch work.
  5. SenseiCoach other developers, host live builds, review work, and help juniors grow.

Sensei tier ladder

More contribution means a higher tier. Each tier begins at a points total.

  1. Tier 1You have started well. You help builders on a regular basis.From 10,000 pts
  2. Tier 2You give steady, reliable help over many weeks.From 25,000 pts
  3. Tier 3You are a leader in the community and others learn from you.From 50,000 pts

Rewards for each tier are being finalised. Nothing is promised until they are announced here.

How you contribute

Real ways to help others become developers, build products, and grow in confidence.

  • Host live builds

    Plan and run live sessions where a real product is built in front of the community.

  • Share your screen while building

    Work in the open, explain your choices, and solve problems out loud.

  • Coach junior developers

    Guide, unblock, and help new developers turn ideas into working products.

  • Review roadmaps and launches

    Give clear feedback on plans, progress and launch readiness.

  • Run office hours

    Hold focused question and answer times or one-to-one help for builders.

How you level up

Grow your impact and move up the Sensei tiers.

  • Earn certifications

    Build trust and show your skill.

  • Complete product builds

    Ship real products and show you can finish the job.

  • Mentor consistently

    Show up, support builders, and make steady impact.

  • Climb the tiers

    Collect points as you contribute and move up the tier ladder.

Contribution is measured by

  • Time spent helping others

    Live sessions, mentoring and office hours.

  • Certifications earned

    Skills and product knowledge that have been checked.

  • Taking part in live work

    Live builds, reviews and real-time support.

How to apply to become a Sensei

Sensei applications are not open yet. This is how it will work, so you can get ready.

Who can apply

  • You are a certified Master Developer, or the Developer Dojo team has invited you.
  • You can show real products or real help you have given to others.
  • You agree to the Code of conduct and to disclose AI use.
  1. Check you can applyYou are a certified Master Developer, or you have been invited by the Developer Dojo team.
  2. Tell us about your workShare what you have built, who you have helped, and which kind of help you want to give.
  3. Talk with the teamA short conversation about how coaching works here and our Code of conduct.
  4. Start with a first sessionYou begin with one supported session. Your points are counted from then on.

Applications open soon. There is no application form yet and we do not list an email address here. To be told when applications open, or to ask a question first, use the contact page.
