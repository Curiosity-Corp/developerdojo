Program sponsor

Reveltek sponsors Developer Dojo

Reveltek sponsors the Developer Dojo program. Their support is what lets Curiosity Research Corporation offer the training, the Library and the challenges free to anyone.

Sponsors support the program. They do not buy scores, certificates or places in a challenge. Every member's work is judged the same way, by the same rubric.

Become a sponsor

Help free training reach more people

Developer Dojo is free for learners because organisations like yours help. There are four ways to take part.

  • Sponsor the program

    Help keep Developer Dojo free. This supports the Library, the live sessions and the review of members’ work.

  • Sponsor a challenge

    Bring a real brief for a Build Sprint. Members build it with AI agents and you see real approaches.

  • Give time as a mentor or reviewer

    Experienced developers can review Product Packages and help members as Sensei.

  • Give tools or services

    Free accounts, credits or hosting that help members build and ship. In-kind gifts are welcome.

How to start

  1. Open the Curiosity Research Corporation contact form (it opens on the CRC website).
  2. Use the subject Sponsor Developer Dojo and tell us who you are and which of the four ways interests you.
  3. We reply to talk through what is possible. Nothing is agreed until it is written down and both sides say yes.

A note on tax: CRC's 501(c)(3) application is pending. We do not describe gifts as tax-deductible. See About CRC.
