Session types

Four kinds of live session

Every session is free. This is what each one is, so you know what to expect when they are scheduled.

  • Live Build

    A Sensei builds a real product step by step, live, and explains each decision. You watch, ask, and build along.

  • Demo Day

    Members show finished work: a Product Package or a Build Sprint result. Short demos, honest feedback.

  • Workshop

    A focused, hands-on session on one skill, such as writing an epic or briefing an AI agent.

  • Office Hours

    Open time with a Sensei. Bring a question, a stuck issue, or a draft epic.
