# Pocket Invoice: moderated usability session sheet

A blank sheet for one session with one participant, using the clickable prototype. Generated from inputs/usability-tasks.json; print it or copy it, and keep the filled-in copy private.

Before the session: ask for consent to take notes, say that the design is being tested and not the participant, and record no name, email address or other personal data on this sheet. Read each scenario as written and do not name the button.

| Task | Scenario (read aloud) | Start screen | Completed? (yes, with help, no) | Steps taken (shortest: n) | What to watch for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | You have just heard about Pocket Invoice. Get set up so you can start invoicing. | Create your workspace |   |  (shortest: 1) | Does the participant understand what a workspace is before they name one? |   |
| T2 | Send your first invoice, for a menu redesign you finished, to the client who asked for it. | Create your workspace |   |  (shortest: 4) | Does the participant check the invoice before sending, or look for a way to skip it? |   |
| T3 | You pressed Check invoice too early and the page shows problems. Get the invoice sent anyway. | New invoice: fix two problems |   |  (shortest: 2) | Does the participant use the links in the error summary, or scroll to find the fields? |   |
| T4 | The invoice did not go out. Find out why and send it again. | The invoice was not sent |   |  (shortest: 3) | Does the participant trust that the other fields were kept? |   |
| T5 | You started an invoice by mistake. Throw it away and go back to your list. | New invoice |   |  (shortest: 2) | Does the participant expect the dialog, and is Discard clear about what is lost? |   |
| T6 | On the check screen you notice the amount is wrong. Fix it and come back to check again. | Check the invoice |   |  (shortest: 2) | Does the participant expect the other values to still be there? |   |
| T7 | You already have a Pocket Invoice account. Get to the place where you would sign in. | Create your workspace |   |  (shortest: 1) | Does the participant find the way to sign in without filling in the form first? |   |

After the session: list each problem with the task, the screen and what the participant said or did. A problem seen with one participant is a lead to check, not a finding.
