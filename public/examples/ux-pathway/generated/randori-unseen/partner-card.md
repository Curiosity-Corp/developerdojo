# UX Randori, round U: the sealed card

For the partner only. Do not show it until the learner has drawn a first sitemap.

**The constraint.** Nothing takes effect until the volunteer has seen every choice they made on one screen and confirmed it there, and going back from that screen keeps every choice.

**What the script checks.** Every action that takes effect starts from a review screen that has no field of its own and shows a summary, including every value typed on the screen that leads to it; the review screen has a way back that does not take effect, and every screen with fields that it goes back to still holds every value.

**Your part.** Keep time. Show the card at the right moment and say nothing more about it. When the prototype is wired, walk it without help, one task from the brief at a time, thinking aloud, and write down where you went and where you stopped. Then sign the review sheet.
