# UX Randori, round U: Kitchen Share

For the learner. Read this, then start: you have 60 minutes and no assistant. Your partner holds one more card and will show it to you after you has drawn a first sitemap.

**The brief.** A community kitchen: a volunteer books a two-hour cooking slot, is told when the slot clashes with another booking, and can swap to a free slot or give the slot up.

**Who uses it.** A volunteer cook who books the shared kitchen on a phone, often in a hurry between shifts.

**Their job.** Get a two-hour slot in the kitchen, and change or give it up when plans change.

**Your flow must include**

- the clash: what the volunteer sees when the slot they want is taken, and how they get to a free one
- giving a slot up, and what happens to it
- at least one error state with its way back

**What to hand in.** A first sitemap (before the card), the adapted sitemap and low-fidelity screens, wired as a clickable prototype, and both sitemaps as flow files. Write the sitemap as a flow file in the same format as inputs/pocket-invoice-flow.json: screens, states with their elements, start, and transitions. Mark each transition that takes effect (book, swap, give up) with "commits": true. Give every field the value the volunteer typed or chose.

Kitchen Share is invented for practice. Use invented names and times only; no real kitchen, person or booking.

No answer to this round is published. The rubric (rubric.md) says how it is reviewed and does not reveal the card; you may read it first. Do not read partner-card.md, or the constraint checks in tools/ux_randori.mjs, before your round: the card is only unseen if you keep it so.
