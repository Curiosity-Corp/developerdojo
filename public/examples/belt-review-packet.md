# Independent practice review packet

This blank packet supports a formative practice review. It is not a belt, certificate, professional approval, or an application. The Dojo does not assign a reviewer or receive this file. Keep it private; share it only with an independent person who agrees to review it and can handle the material appropriately. For an actual Dojo certificate, follow the existing rank-specific assessment and certificate procedure.
