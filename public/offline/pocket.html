POCKET DOJO

Practice anywhere.

A small offline pack. Three prompts, your notes, and no account.

Your progress is saved only on this device when JavaScript and browser storage are available.

1. Frame the outcome

Choose something you want to develop. Write who it is for and one way you will check that it works.

2. Find the weak claim

Practice data: 10 people visited a fictional page; 2 signed up. A draft says “everyone signed up.” What can you actually conclude?

Reveal one check

Two of ten signed up, or 20%. The data does not say what the other visitors thought or why they did not sign up.

3. Adapt the plan

Your preferred tool is unavailable. What could you use instead, and how would you verify that the result still meets your goal?
