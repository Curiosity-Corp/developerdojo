## How to fill

How to fill the Repository map

1. Start from the product's GitLab groups and the domains you can open in a browser. List every one.

2. Type: pick from the list. Use 'Marketing site' for the public website. 'App' for the product people log in to. 'Squeeze page or interactive tool' for lead pages, briefs and calculators.

'Azure microservice' = a Logic App or Function, often behind Azure Front Door. 'Integration' = glue to another vendor. 'CRM', 'Payments', 'Ops' are their own types.

3. 'What it ties into' = which other rows it talks to. Write the names from column A. This is the most important column: your epics come from these links.

4. Owner = a real person or team. If nobody owns it, write 'NOBODY' and look at it again in Week 3. That is a candidate epic.

5. If a part you expect does not exist (for example no payment gateway), add the row and write 'NOT FOUND' in Notes. A missing part is a finding.

6. Finish with a one-page picture of the whole product (boxes and arrows). Add it as a new sheet or a picture.
