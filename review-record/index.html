Ranks · specialist belts · transfers · independent review

Record an independent review

For the reviewer in a pilot review: record each criterion against real evidence, then copy the review log. Nothing leaves your device.

Start recordingWhat each rank and belt asks

A record, not an award: Finishing a review record issues no belt and no rank, and the rubrics it uses are proposed, not approved. A rank certificate still follows the certificate process, with a separate Maintainer deciding. Dojo assessment is not professional licensure or accreditation. This website takes no authenticated submissions: the record stays on your device until you copy it.

How to use it

  1. Before the session, agree scope, access and consent with the learner in writing, and check you are independent. The form will not finish without both.
  2. Choose what you review: a rank rubric, a specialist belt in one field at the Brown or Black stage, or a learner's transfer of the foundation technique into one field from the mission playbook. The criteria and, for a belt or a transfer, the field's own limit appear below. For a transfer you also see the field's reference, its review gate, the checks to rerun and the kind of unseen change to set.
  3. Set the unseen task when the work starts, then open the original artifact and observe.
  4. Record every criterion as Observed, Not yet observed or Unclear, each with an evidence reference: a file, a check result, a log line or a moment in the session. Absence of evidence is not a pass, so the form will not finish without a reference.
  5. Finish and copy the review log into the confidential case issue. A finished record is locked; a mistake is fixed with a dated correction added below it, never by editing.

Record a review

For the reviewer. What you type is saved only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is no account. Do not type the learner's name, passwords or private material: refer to evidence by file, check or moment in the session.

Developer rank

Can this person develop one outcome from start to finish and show evidence that it works?

DEV-1 Outcome and checks

Observed when: The learner states the audience, the intended result and the checks before building, and the artifact passes those checks when the reviewer runs them.

Not yet observed when: The checks are written after the result, are missing, or only the learner has run them.

Look at: The actual artifacts · Tests and checks

DEV-2 Reproducible stack

Observed when: The learner records the harness, model, instructions, tools, versions and permissions actually used, and the reviewer can repeat the main run from that record.

Not yet observed when: The stack is described in general terms, or the reviewer cannot repeat the run from the record.

Look at: Reproducible configuration · The actual artifacts

DEV-3 Failures on the record

Observed when: At least one real failure or wrong turn is recorded with what caused it, what changed and which check confirmed the repair.

Not yet observed when: The record shows only successes, or a failure is mentioned without the check that closed it.

Look at: Failures · Tests and checks · Review log

Master Developer rank

Can this person carry their judgment to a tool family or field they did not practice in?

MD-1 Transfer to an unseen tool family or field

Observed when: Given a brief the learner has not seen, in a tool family or field other than the one practiced, the learner chooses and adapts a stack, and the result passes checks the reviewer chose.

Not yet observed when: The learner repeats a practiced recipe, stays in the practiced tool family, or only the learner checks the result.

Look at: The actual artifacts · Reproducible configuration · Tests and checks · Decision evidence

MD-2 Recovery from a changed tool or failure

Observed when: When the reviewer withdraws a tool or injects a failure during the task, the learner diagnoses it, changes the approach, repeats the same checks and records what changed.

Not yet observed when: The learner restarts from scratch without a diagnosis, drops a check to get a pass, or hides the failure.

Look at: Failures · Tests and checks · Review log

MD-3 Decisions defended without notes

Observed when: In the review the learner explains each main decision, the alternative they rejected, which check proves what, and what is still unverified, and adapts the plan when the reviewer adds a constraint.

Not yet observed when: Decisions are explained only as what an agent suggested, or the learner cannot say what remains unverified.

Look at: Decision evidence · Review log

Sensei rank

Can this person teach and review a technique that works in many fields, and show how it adapts in each?

SEN-1 Teach a generalizable technique

Observed when: Another learner reproduces the technique from the Sensei's explanation, then meets an unseen constraint the reviewer chose, and the Sensei coaches without doing the work for them.

Not yet observed when: The other learner can only follow the exact steps, or the Sensei takes over when the constraint changes.

Look at: The actual artifacts · Review log · Decision evidence

SEN-2 Domain adaptations

Observed when: The Sensei shows how the technique changes in a second field the reviewer names: the different inputs, tools, checks and the qualified review that field requires.

Not yet observed when: The technique is presented as identical in every field, or the field's own checks and approvals are missing.

Look at: Decision evidence · Reproducible configuration · Tests and checks

SEN-3 Independent review of another person's work

Observed when: Given a packet they did not prepare, with a known defect added by the pilot coordinator, the Sensei records each criterion as observed, not yet observed or unclear, with evidence references, and finds the defect or says what stopped them.

Not yet observed when: The review repeats the learner's own claims, gives a score without evidence references, or shares material without consent.

Look at: Review log · Failures · Decision evidence

Transfer into SaaS UX

Can this person carry the foundation technique into this field, check the work the way the field checks it, and keep it working when the situation changes?

Qualified review in this field: Not required for this invented practice product, and none was done. The automated checks are not an accessibility audit, and nothing here is a conformance statement for a real product.

What you check the transfer against

The field's independent reference
The flow and the done-when list for each screen, and contrast ratios computed from the design tokens.
Its review gate
“You compare every line with the flow and the done-when list before it goes into Penpot.” (Drafting assistant, UX-001 Map a sitemap with branches and error states)
Checks you rerun on the learner's work
  • The Penpot file is rebuilt from the flow and tokens, and every token colour pair meets its contrast ratio
  • The working example is driven through every state with axe, reflow and focus checks, and the clickable prototype through every transition
  • The flow file is redrawn as a sitemap, a one-canvas journey and one low-fidelity screen per state, and rechecked for reachability, dead ends, error recovery, dialogs, button wiring and token contrast
  • The same script draws the style guide, the component sheet and every state at 390 px and 1440 px in high fidelity from the tokens and the brand file (tools/ux_hifi.mjs), and audits each drawing for token use, text and boundary contrast, control size and component coverage
  • The same script checks the worked UX Randori rounds (tools/ux_randori.mjs): both sitemaps of each round pass every structural check, the adapted one passes the check for its hidden constraint, and the first one fails it
The unseen change you set
One the learner has not practised, of the kind in UX-R01 UX Randori: an unseen flow with a hidden constraint. Not a published card as written.
What the learner worked from
The SaaS UX practice sheet. Its self-check is the learner's own and is not evidence for a criterion.
