Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is membership really free?

Yes. There is no fee, no trial, no card to enter and no paid tier. Developer Dojo is run by Curiosity Research Corporation and sponsored by Reveltek, so training stays free for anyone.

Do I need to give you my name or email?

No. This website has no sign-up form and collects no personal data. Your account is with GitLab or GitHub, not with us. If you contact us or submit a challenge, you choose what to share.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. A developer can orchestrate an agent stack to develop outcomes in many fields. Start with curiosity and a clear goal; build the knowledge and verification skills your field needs.

My English is not perfect. Is that a problem?

No. We write in plain words on purpose, and we ask you to write in your own plain words too. Clear thinking matters more than perfect grammar.

Is there a certificate?

The current product program describes certificates for Developer, Master Developer and Sensei. A certificate appears in the public registry only when it has been issued. Domain belts are being developed; neither is a professional license.

Can I use AI tools?

Yes. You learn to choose and equip agents and verify their work. The existing product curriculum asks people to write epics and roadmaps and disclose AI use; see The Way. Program policy is being reviewed as the Dojo expands.

Where is the community?

The community space is opening soon. We will not point you to a place that is not ready. This page will link it when it opens.

How should members behave?

Be kind, be specific, and be patient with learners. Read the code of conduct.
