UX pathway · Working example

Pocket Invoice

A freelancer sends their first invoice to a client. Pocket Invoice is invented for practice: it is not a product, and nothing you type leaves this page. Nothing is saved; reload the page to start again.

Try the errors as well as the happy path: leave a field empty, type an amount of 0, or give a client email ending in .invalid to see a failed send. Everything works with a keyboard.
