# UX Randori: worked rounds

Generated by tools/ux_randori.mjs (called by tools/ux_pathway.mjs) from inputs/randori-rounds.json. Do not edit: change the input and rerun the script.

Each round answers one partner card from the UX-R01 download twice: the first sitemap, drawn before the hidden constraint was revealed, and the adapted one that meets it. Both must pass every structural check of the flow script; the adapted one must pass the check for its constraint, and the first must fail it, or the constraint changed nothing.

**Not unseen for the authors.** The maintainers who wrote the cards also wrote these answers, so the constraint was never hidden from them. No partner has walked these flows. Use the rounds after your own session, to compare, not as a model to copy first.

## Round A: The user must be able to undo the last action for 30 seconds.

**Brief:** A library app: a member reserves a book, is told when it is ready, and can cancel.

**What the constraint changed (in the author’s words):** Reserving and cancelling now show Undo for 30 seconds. The confirmation dialog before cancelling is gone: Undo protects the user better, with one step fewer.

**Check:** Every action that takes effect leads to a state that offers Undo for at least 30 seconds.

Before the constraint, the check found:

- book > reserved: reserved offers no Undo for 30 seconds
- book > reserved: no Undo transition leaves reserved
- cancel-dialog > cancelled: cancelled offers no Undo for 30 seconds
- cancel-dialog > cancelled: no Undo transition leaves cancelled

After: no problems.

## Round C: The user may lose their connection at any step; nothing they typed may be lost.

**Brief:** A class trip form: a parent gives permission, pays, and can change their answer.

**What the constraint changed (in the author’s words):** Each screen with a field gets an offline state that shows the answers kept on the device and a way on once the connection is back: Try again sends them, Back returns to the form.

**Check:** Every state with a field has an offline state that keeps each field and its value, with a way back or on once the connection returns.

- permission: no offline state; what was typed would be lost
- pay: no offline state; what was typed would be lost

## Not checked here

- Whether a person can use the adapted flow. That is the partner walk in the kata: a moderated test, with notes on where they stopped.
- The two keyboard and reading constraints on the cards (focus order, one action per screen) are about the screens, not the flow, so no flow check can prove them.
- Whether the 30-second Undo, the approval or the offline copy work in software. These are flows and wireframes, not a working app.
