# Walk-through script: Product discovery (pilot)

Track page: https://developerdojo.org/library/tracks/product-discovery/
Status: script ready for review. Not recorded yet. See [Track walk-through videos](../track-videos.md).

## Script (about 300 words)

**Who it is for.**
Hello. This is a short tour of the Product discovery track on Developer Dojo.
Most products fail for a boring reason. Someone built the wrong thing very well.
Discovery is the work you do to avoid that.
This track is for you if you want to own a product, not only a part of it.
You may be a junior developer, a career changer or a designer who wants to lead.
You may be an engineer who now steers AI agents.
You do not need to code to start.
You do need to talk to real people and change your mind when the evidence says so.

**What you can do afterwards.**
The track has four lessons.
First, you frame an idea as a user, a problem, one number and a date.
Second, you plan and run five customer interviews and keep private details out of git.
Third, you draw the whole product on one page, with every system it depends on.
Fourth, you turn evidence into a decision you can defend: build, change, test further or stop.
Every lesson uses the same fictional product, TrailMug, reusable cups for cafés.
Every lesson ends with a file in your own git repository.

**Where AI fits.**
Agents can draft an interview guide or tidy your notes. You check every result.
You never ask an AI to invent customers, quotes or findings.
In the Dojo you also say how you used AI.
The decisions stay with you: what is worth learning, what counts as proof, and what you build.

**The first lesson to open.**
Start with lesson one, Frame the problem and the idea. Plan about ninety minutes.
Write your one-paragraph brief before you watch anything else.
Then come back to the track page for lesson two.
Thank you for watching.
