Three ranks

Developer, Master Developer, Sensei

The existing product program describes a certificate for each rank. These requirements remain in place as the wider Dojo evolves.

  1. Developer

    You own one product from the first customer conversation to a launch plan, and you can show it.

    What you need

    • Finish the six-week Developer path.
    • Hand in a Product Package: eight parts, kept in git.
    • Present it in 15 minutes and answer questions without notes.
    • Reach level 3 on all six skills in the rubric, with none below level 2.

    Product Package standard Product Package rubric

  2. Master Developer

    You can run the ground products stand on, launch safely, and steer AI agents across several repositories.

    What you need

    • Study the Master Developer path: platform, open-source-first choices, GitOps and service management.
    • Pass the four gates, each with a written test and an observed practical.
    • Pass the final mixed exam and the oral review.
    • Score at least 70 points and finish every mandatory item.

    Master Developer assessment

  3. Sensei

    You coach others, host live builds and review work. Sensei is by application, not by exam.

    What you need

    • Hold a Master Developer certificate, or be invited by the Developer Dojo team.
    • Apply and talk with the team about how coaching works here.
    • Keep contributing: points and tiers are explained on the Sensei page.

    How to become a Sensei

Rank and specialization

A rank and a belt answer different questions

Developer, Master Developer and Sensei describe growth in development and mentorship. Future specialization belts are intended to show demonstrated ability in a field through evidence and independent review. The belt system is still being developed and no domain belts are issued here today.

Neither a Dojo certificate nor a future belt is a professional license, permit or authorization. Regulated and safety-critical work requires the applicable independent validation and qualified approval.

Assessment

How assessment works

The existing product program uses evidence in git and human review. The wider kata and belt assessment process is being developed.

  • Gates

    Master Developer candidates pass four gates in order. Each has a written test and a practical task that someone watches.

  • Product Package

    Developer candidates hand in one folder in git that proves they own a whole product. Reviewers look at evidence, not at promises.

  • Oral review

    You explain your choices out loud, without notes. Questions test judgement: why do people write the epics, why do we fix forward.

  • Integrity and AI policy

    In the existing product curriculum, people write epics and roadmaps and disclose AI use. Program policy is being reviewed as the Dojo expands.

Read the full rules: Master Developer assessment, Product Package standard, why people write epics and how to disclose AI use.

Validity

Certificates are renewed every year

Tools and good practice change, so a certificate is valid for one year. To renew, your recent work is reviewed again. A certificate that has not been renewed after a year is no longer valid.

Verify a certificate

Type the certificate ID printed on the certificate, or the holder's name. We check it against the public list of certificates on this page.

How verification works
  • A certificate is real only if it appears in this public list. The list is part of this site's source code in git, so every change is recorded.
  • We list a person only when they agreed to be listed.
  • A name can be shared by more than one person. Check the ID, the rank and the date too.
  • If you are shown a certificate that you cannot find here, treat it as unverified. Contact us with the ID and we will check.
