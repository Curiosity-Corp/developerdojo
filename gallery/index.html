One prototype brief, three stacks, twelve checked runs

The FND-001 comparison record. The same prototype brief went to an AI coding assistant with one variable changed at a time (the instruction, then the model). An independent browser checker scored every response, and failed runs were corrected and checked again.

  • Read the evidence notes
  • Every check result (JSON)
  • Open one checked run
Artifact
Run records (JSON), the prompts sent, the HTML of every run and the check results. The actual file, served here. Open it, inspect it and edit a copy.
Made by
Developer Dojo maintainers, November 20, 2026. Claude Code 2.1.329 in print mode with claude-sonnet-5-5 and claude-haiku-5-5, no tools and no inherited configuration. The runner and checker scripts are published with the site source.
Source
Prompt A from the "Make a clickable prototype" kata. Nine first runs and three corrections, all on 2026-11-20.
Rights
Dojo material under the MIT licence. The model responses are kept unedited as evidence. Read the terms
Checked
Every pipeline re-runs the browser checker on all twelve stored responses and the reference, and fails if any recorded result changes. The counts, times and costs in the record are recomputed from the run files. Negative controls show the checker fails broken files. See the evidence
Not checked
Three runs per arm on one task, one date and one harness version: observations to repeat, not a benchmark. Costs are list prices reported by the tool, not an invoice. The harness, skill, tool and delegation comparisons were designed but not run.
Outcome
Compare two AI stacks fairly
Fields
Software and digital products

Make your own

  1. RecipeNot available yet. The FND-001 recipe page is not published yet. The evidence notes give its setup, smoke test and checks. Practise the same task in the stack workbench
  2. Exercise Make a clickable prototype
  3. QA
    • Automated check: Every response is checked by an independent browser checker (scripts/fnd-001/check-pocket-garden.mjs) that never reads the model's own checklist. CI re-runs it on the stored responses and fails if a recorded result changes.
    • Self-check: The learner records harness, model, versions, settings, every check result, time and cost (or "not shown") for each run, before reading the other run.
    • Peer review: A second person reads the learner's record and repeats at least two checks on the saved HTML.
  4. Belt
    • Practice: Runs the smoke test and the baseline, records harness, model and versions (or "not shown"), and scores the saved HTML against every rubric check.
    • Adapt: Changes exactly one variable, keeps the rest as stable as the tools allow, repeats each arm at least three times and names the confounders it could not remove.
    • Demonstrate: Completes the unseen transfer challenge: writes the observable checks before prompting, runs a controlled comparison and reports failures and corrections without overstating the result.
  5. FoundationsThis is a shared foundation itself: every field builds on it.

  • Verified: Re-checked in CI, November 20, 2026

An IFC model changed and checked again against IDS

The BIM-004 worked example. A buildingSMART IFC4 sample fails one of six practice IDS requirements; a scoped IfcOpenShell edit fills the missing wall property, keeps all 24 GlobalIds, and the IDS, schema and geometry checks are rerun on the copy and reported separately.

  • Download the edited model (IFC)
  • Read the check summary
  • Open the practice requirements (IDS)
Artifact
IFC4 model (edited copy), IDS 1.0 requirements and the check results as JSON. The actual file, served here. Open it, inspect it and edit a copy.
Made by
Developer Dojo maintainers (edit, requirements and checks); buildingSMART International (source model), November 21, 2026. IfcOpenShell 0.9.0.post1 and IfcTester 0.9.0 on Python 3.12, run by the pathway script published next to the model.
Source
The buildingSMART "wall with opening and window" IFC4 sample, pinned to one commit of its certification datasets.
Rights
The edited model is an adaptation of © buildingSMART International under CC BY 4.0. The requirements, script and results are Dojo material under the MIT licence. Read the terms
Checked
Every pipeline installs the pinned packages by hash, rebuilds the edited model and reruns every check, and fails if any output differs from the published files. Original: 5 of 6 IDS requirements pass. Edited: 6 of 6, no schema issues with EXPRESS rules off, and all three shapes build. See the evidence
Not checked
Practice requirements on a three-element test file: nothing about a real building. "REI 60" is a placeholder, not a fire rating. No EXPRESS where-rules, no authoring tool or viewer, no learner and no professional review.
Outcome
Change a building model and check it again
Fields
Architecture and BIM, Construction and infrastructure

Make your own

  1. RecipeChange the model and check it again
  2. Exercise Follow the BIM pathway
  3. QA
    • Automated check: An edited IFC copy with exactly one changed property and every GlobalId kept, matching the Dojo's CI output
    • Automated check: The IDS, schema and geometry checks rerun on the edited copy, reported separately

    Real-world use of this outcome needs review by a qualified professional. Practice here does not replace it.

  4. Belt
    • Practice: Reproduces the edit and gets 6 of 6 IDS passes, no schema issues and all shapes built
    • Adapt: Makes a different edit and proves with a diff of properties that nothing else changed
    • Demonstrate: Writes a short change record that a model author could review, naming what was checked and who must still approve it

Pocket Invoice, an editable Penpot file from sitemap to high fidelity

A Penpot file for an invented invoicing app: a sitemap with every error state, wired low- and high-fidelity screens for all 11 states, design tokens and 8 components. A working page built from the same flow and tokens sits next to it.

  • Download the Penpot file
  • Download the design tokens (JSON)
  • Use the working page
Artifact
Penpot file (.penpot, a ZIP of JSON) and a design token file (JSON). The actual file, served here. Open it, inspect it and edit a copy.
Made by
Developer Dojo maintainers, November 20, 2026. Generated from the Pocket Invoice flow and token file with Penpot's official library, @penpot/library 1.3.0, by a script written with an AI coding assistant. The generator is published with the site source.
Source
An invented product and fictional data. The error summary follows the GOV.UK Design System pattern (Open Government Licence v3.0); no GOV.UK text or code is copied.
Rights
Dojo material under the MIT licence. Read the terms
Checked
Imported into a self-hosted Penpot 2.18.7: 4 pages, 11 state boards per wireframe page, 8 components, 16 colours, 5 typographies and all 54 tokens, and a 22-click View mode walk of both prototypes reached all 19 transitions. Every pipeline rebuilds the file and fails if it differs from the published copy or loses a board, an interaction or a token. See the evidence
Not checked
Penpot cloud, editing by a person, export to other formats, and writing through a plugin or the MCP server. No usability sessions have been run.

A maize irrigation estimate from a real weather record

The AGR-003 worked example. A year of NOAA daily weather from a California station becomes FAO-56 reference evapotranspiration and a maize crop water and irrigation estimate, after the script reproduces FAO’s own worked examples.

  • Read the results summary
  • Download the irrigation estimate (JSON)
  • Download the daily evapotranspiration (CSV)
Artifact
Monthly irrigation estimate (JSON), daily reference evapotranspiration (CSV) and a one-page summary (Markdown). The actual file, served here. Open it, inspect it and edit a copy.
Made by
Developer Dojo maintainers (script and results); US National Weather Service via NOAA NCEI (weather record), November 21, 2026. A Python 3.12 script with no packages, published next to the results, using the equations of FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56.
Source
The 2023 GHCN-Daily record of the Fresno Yosemite International station (USW00093193), as NOAA’s data service returned it.
Rights
The weather record is a US federal government work, not under copyright in the US. The script and results are Dojo material under the MIT licence. Read the terms
Checked
Every pipeline reruns the script, which first reproduces 13 published values from FAO’s worked examples, and fails if any output differs from the published files. See the evidence
Not checked
Humidity, radiation and wind are estimated from temperature, not measured. FAO table crop values, not adjusted for this site. No real field, soil or irrigation system, no learner and no agronomist or irrigation engineer review.
Outcome
Estimate a crop’s irrigation need
Fields
Agriculture and natural resources

Make your own

  1. RecipeEstimate a crop's net irrigation need from crop coefficients and effective rain
  2. Exercise Follow the agriculture pathway
  3. QA
    • Automated check: Code that reproduces the six effective-rainfall answers in FAO Training Manual 3
    • Automated check: A monthly table of ETc, rain, effective rain and net irrigation need that matches the Dojo's CI output
    • Sensei review: A Sensei or partner checks that the learner states what the net need leaves out

    Real-world use of this outcome needs review by a qualified professional. Practice here does not replace it.

  4. Belt
    • Practice: Reproduces the monthly irrigation table for maize and explains why effective rain is zero every month
    • Adapt: Estimates another crop from FAO-56 Tables 11 and 12, naming the rows used
    • Demonstrate: Explains to a reviewer why the net need is not the water to order, listing what a real estimate adds
  5. FoundationsCompare AI instructions, Check context and trust
