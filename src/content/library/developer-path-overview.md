The Developer path is the first rank of the dojo. In six weeks you take **one product** from idea to a package you can present. You do not just write code. You talk to customers, plan the whole product, and steer AI agents that do the building.

## What you need

- A product idea you care about. It can be small. You may use a real idea, or the fictional TrailMug used in these pages.
- About 8 to 10 hours a week.
- A free account on a git host such as gitlab.com or github.com.
- A coach or mentor if you can find one, or a study partner.

## The six weeks

| Week | Theme | You finish with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [See the whole product](/library/week-1-see-the-whole-product) | A one-page map of the product and customer interviews started |
| 2 | [Know the people](/library/week-2-know-the-people) | Personas, touch points and journeys |
| 3 | [From problems to epics](/library/week-3-problems-to-epics) | A roadmap of 4 to 8 epics, written by you |
| 4 | [Defend the roadmap](/library/week-4-defend-the-roadmap) | A reviewed roadmap, stakeholder map and agent-ready epics |
| 5 | [Brand, website and mockups](/library/week-5-brand-website-mockups) | A brand name, a simple website and next-version mockups |
| 6 | [Catalogue, demo and final package](/library/week-6-catalogue-demo-package) | A catalogue, a demo and your Product Package presentation |

## The final assessment

The last week ends with a **Product Package**. It is a bundle of evidence that you own the product. See [Product Package standard](/library/product-package-standard) and [Product Package rubric](/library/product-package-rubric).

## How to study each week

1. Read the week page.
2. Do the exercises in your own project, in git.
3. Use the templates in the Templates section.
4. Pick two or three items from the [Learning path](/library/learning-path-how-to-use).
5. On Friday, fill in the self-check and write a [weekly note](/library/template-weekly-note).

## The rules still apply

All five rules of [The Way](/library/the-way-five-rules) apply from day one. The most important for you: **you write your own epics**, and **you say how you used AI**.

## Passing

You pass when you reach level 3 ("Competent") on all rubric skills and no skill is below level 2. If you do not, you get a coaching plan, not a failure.

## After the Developer path

Next ranks: Master Developer (platform, production launch, AI-agent orchestration) and Sensei (coaching, hosting live builds, reviewing work).
