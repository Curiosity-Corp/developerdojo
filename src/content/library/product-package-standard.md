The **Product Package** is the final assessment of the Developer path. It is one folder in git and one presentation. It proves that you own a product from end to end.

## The eight parts

| # | Part | What it must show |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Current state** | What exists today, honestly: what works, what is a plan, what is missing. Use FOUND, NOT FOUND and PLANNED. |
| 2 | **Product website** | A public page that explains the product, who it is for and how to start. Source in git. |
| 3 | **Brand name** | The name, the one-sentence promise, the brand sheet and why you chose it. |
| 4 | **Product and service catalogue** | Your [Product Catalogue](/library/product-catalogue-standard) and a catalogue entry for each product or service. |
| 5 | **Roadmap** | 4 to 8 epics in order, with dates, reasons and an out-of-scope list. |
| 6 | **Demo** | A five-minute walk-through of the real journey with fake data. |
| 7 | **Next-version mockups** | Three or more mockups, each linked to an epic. |
| 8 | **Evidence of complete epic management** | History of your epics, moved dates with reasons, decision log, weekly notes, closed milestones and AI-use notes. |

## Folder layout

```text
product-package/
  README.md              start here: one paragraph and links
  01-current-state.md
  02-website/            link and source
  03-brand/              brand sheet
  04-catalogue/          master, orbital, android, tech, entries
  05-roadmap/            epics and order
  06-demo/               link or script
  07-mockups/
  08-evidence/           notes, decision log, ai-use
```

## The presentation

15 minutes plus questions:

1. 1 minute: the product and the customer.
2. 2 minutes: current state.
3. 3 minutes: demo.
4. 4 minutes: roadmap, order, risks and what you chose not to do.
5. 2 minutes: catalogue and brand.
6. 1 minute: how you used AI.
7. 2 minutes: next-version mockups.

Then answer questions without notes.

## What reviewers look for

- Is it your thinking?
- Does it cover the whole product, not just one part?
- Can agents build from your epics?
- Is it honest about what is not finished?

## Rules

- Use fake data in the demo and in screenshots.
- No secrets in the folder.
- Disclose AI use in `08-evidence/ai-use.md`.
- Every claim is something you verified.

## Key points

- Eight parts, one folder, one presentation.
- Honesty beats polish.
- Score yourself with the [rubric](/library/product-package-rubric) before you present.
