## Working with AI agents

An agent summarised Noor's notes. Brief: "Group these notes by theme and quote the line for each. Do not add themes." Noor read three originals and found it had merged "cost" and "trust". She split them. An agent **may** summarise notes you took. It **may not** invent personas, quotes or steps, or fill the feeling column. Say so in your weekly note.

## The 2030 Product Development Manager lens

- **You decide:** who to interview, who is customer and who is user, and what each persona says.
- **You delegate:** summaries of your own notes, with the brief above.
- **You verify by:** reading three original notes against the summary.
- **Disciplines involved:** research, design, sales, support and privacy.
- **On the agent ladder:** **use**.
