---
title: "Template: Weekly note"
description: "A short note that tells your sponsor or coach the truth about the week."
section: "Templates"
order: 10
---

Send it every Friday. Keep it short. Be honest. A true red status is useful.

```text
Weekly note: <product>, week <N>, <date>
From: <epic manager>   To: <sponsor or coach>

1. Status: green / yellow / red (one word, one reason)
2. Done this week (max 3 lines, outcomes not tasks)
3. Next week goals (3 lines)
4. Blocked (what, by whom, since when, what I need)
5. Decisions I made (one line each, with reason)
6. Decisions I need from you (max 2)
7. Risks (top 3, with owner)
8. One thing I learned from a user
9. Roadmap changes (epic, old date, new date, why)
10. AI use this week (what it did, what I checked)
```

## Example

```text
Weekly note: TrailMug, week 3, 2026-03-13
Status: yellow. The email provider is slow to approve our domain.
Done: five interviews finished; three personas written; roadmap draft v1.
Next: epic review with coach; write epics 4 to 6; start landing page.
Blocked: domain approval, waiting 4 days, I need a second contact.
Decisions made: leave payments for epic 5 (no customer asked for it yet).
Need from you: 20 minutes to review my out-of-scope lines.
Risks: provider delay (me); only one cafe interviewed (me).
Learned: three of five users said "dirty cup" is the main worry.
Roadmap changes: epic 3 moved from 20 March to 27 March, waiting on domain.
AI use: fixed grammar in persona sheets; I checked every change.
```
