The learning path is a list of articles, docs, videos and courses chosen to support the dojo. It is **free first**. Paid items are listed separately and are optional.

## How to use it

1. Read the week page in the [Developer path](/library/developer-path-overview).
2. Open the matching week on the learning path.
3. Pick two or three items. Do not try to read everything.
4. Write two lines in your weekly note: what you learned and what you will change.
5. Do the exercise on the week page. Reading alone does not teach.

## The rules of the path

- **Do the items of each week in order.**
- **After each item, write three lines in your own words** about how it changes your product. Reading and watching are not enough.
- **Free first.** Where there is a cost, the list says so.
- **Certificates are optional.** A certificate shows you know the words. Your roadmap and capstone show you can do the job.
- **Prices change.** Confirm on the provider's site before you pay.

See [the learning path, week by week](/library/learning-path-week-by-week) for the two tracks, the hours and the skill tracks that match each week.

## The lists

- [Plan the product](/library/learning-path-plan-the-product): roadmaps, personas, journeys, epics, cloud basics.
- [Platform track](/library/learning-path-platform-track): delivery from git, licences, infrastructure as code, service management.
- [Optional paid courses](/library/learning-path-optional-paid): certificates and courses that may cost money.

## Honest notes

- **Links change.** Each link was checked when the list was made. If one breaks, search for the title and the provider.
- **Hours are estimates.**
- **We do not own these resources.** Each belongs to its provider. Follow their terms.
- **Free means free to read or watch.** A certificate or graded work may cost money.
- **Use AI to explain a word, not to do the exercise.** See [Disclose AI use](/library/disclose-ai-use).

## If you already know a topic

Skip it. The path is a menu. Spend your time on the weeks where your self-score is lowest.
