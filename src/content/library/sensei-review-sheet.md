## 1. Plan (30 minutes)

- [ ] 0 to 3: welcome, plan, "this is a review of the work, not of you"
- [ ] 3 to 8: the learner shows the work and says what they decided and why
- [ ] 8 to 18: I open the evidence, check the **[M]** items, ask the honesty questions
- [ ] 18 to 25: two strengths with evidence, one or two priorities
- [ ] 25 to 29: the learner chooses the next step and writes it down
- [ ] 29 to 30: I say when the written summary comes

## 2. Honesty questions

- [ ] Did you write every epic and roadmap sentence yourself?
- [ ] Where did you use AI, and did you say so? (AI-use note found: yes / no)
- [ ] Can you explain this epic out loud, without notes?
