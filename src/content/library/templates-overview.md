Always make your own copy before you type. Never fill in the original.

## Rules

1. **You write the content.** AI may format and check what you decided. See [People write epics](/library/people-write-epics).
2. **Mark every guess.** Use K for known and G for guess.
3. **No secrets, no real customer data.** Use fake data in examples.
4. **Keep the file in git.** It is the source of truth.
5. **Say how you used AI.** See [Disclose AI use](/library/disclose-ai-use).

The examples use TrailMug, a fictional reusable-cup subscription.
