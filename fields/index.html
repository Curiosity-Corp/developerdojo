Sector plan and what is delivered

Every field above sits in one of ten sector groups. A group in the plan is not a course: the last column shows only what you can follow today. A sector pathway is a run of katas on real, openly licensed files whose results CI recomputes, with a plain list of what was and was not checked.

Sector groupFields in the planDelivered today
Digital products and UXSoftware and digital products, UX and product design, Data and analytics, CybersecuritySector pathway available
  • SaaS UX: from sitemap to a tested screen

Before a next pathway: A data or security pack needs an openly licensed dataset or system to work on, reference results a script can reproduce, and a reviewer for any real security finding.

Spatial design and media3D and spatial design, Media and communicationsIn the plan only, no course yet

Before a first pathway: Needs openly licensed source files (models, captures, footage) whose licence allows changes, and checks a script can rerun on them.

Built environmentArchitecture and BIM, Construction and infrastructureSector pathway available
  • Architecture and BIM: from an IFC file to checked information

Before a next pathway: A construction pack needs open schedule or asset data, and the same qualified-review limits as the BIM pathway.

Industrial and hardwareManufacturing and product design, Aerospace and transportIn the plan only, no course yet

Before a first pathway: Needs an openly licensed part, assembly or process record, a published method with worked examples, and an engineer to review any real use.

Robotics and autonomyRobotics and autonomyRelated track only, no sector pathway yet
  • Robotics and autonomy track

Before a first pathway: Simulation only: an open robot model and scenario, with results CI can rerun, and safety review named for anything on real hardware.

Science and engineeringScience and researchSector pathway available
  • Science and research: from a published record to a result with its uncertainty

Before a next pathway: A next pack (laboratory measurement, for example) needs reference data the Dojo may serve; copyrighted reference sets are cited and linked, never served.

Health and life sciencesBiotechnology and lab automation, Health and life sciencesRelated track only, no sector pathway yet
  • Biotechnology and lab automation track
  • Health and life sciences track

Before a first pathway: Public, non-identifying data only, simulation for any lab work, and a qualified reviewer named for anything near clinical use.

Business and public serviceFinance and operations, Public service and legal operationsIn the plan only, no course yet

Before a first pathway: Fictional or public records only, published rules cited by version and jurisdiction, and an accountant or legal reviewer named for real use.

Energy, agriculture and natural resourcesEnergy and environment, Agriculture and natural resourcesSector pathway available
  • Agriculture: from a weather record to a checked irrigation estimate

Before a next pathway: An energy pack needs a public metered or modelled dataset and a published calculation method with worked examples to reproduce.

Learning and communicationsEducation and learningIn the plan only, no course yet

Before a first pathway: Needs an openly licensed curriculum or assessment framework and a way to check learning materials that does not involve real learners’ data.

Expert review matters

In regulated or safety-critical work, a generated artifact needs independent validation and any approvals required from qualified professionals. Practice in the Dojo does not grant a license or authorize professional work.
